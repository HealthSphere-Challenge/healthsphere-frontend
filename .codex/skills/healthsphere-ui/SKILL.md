## Workflow

Inspect existing components and the actual reference pages before planning a screen. Separate observed design evidence from unresolved tokens; do not silently pick a new font, brand or icon language. Identify reusable primitives and write a screen/state plan tied to the ticket. Preserve English copy and one-account/one-profile scope. Treat persona layouts as adaptation references, not guardian permissions or clinical rules. Implement only after the ticket/plan is authorized; provide reference comparisons and state/responsive evidence for review.

Use the supplied logo without redrawing it. Define one reusable brand component, documented size/clear-space rules, and full or symbol variants only when the actual asset supports them. Organize supplied photography as product assets; never generate replacement people, infer medical conditions, or present photographed people as patients or users.

Aim for a calm, credible and warm healthcare interface: strong typography, generous whitespace, a deliberate grid, restrained radius/shadow, and a small number of purposeful elements. Prefer editorial composition, photography and varied section rhythm. Avoid generic SaaS grids, floating metrics, decorative AI motifs, gradients/glass effects, fake statistics, testimonials, awards, certifications or claims.

Keep AI subordinate to the healthcare-information product. Never introduce diagnosis, treatment claims, an “AI doctor,” clinical validation, fabricated recommendations, or Low/Medium/High and Good/Bad labels for the experimental score. State that assessments are synthetic-data trained, uncalibrated, experimental and non-diagnostic wherever the context could imply more.
